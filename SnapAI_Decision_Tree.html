💡 The Most Important Thing to Understand About This Product
For 14 of the 19 cards, the reading is the diagnosis — not the AI. The logic is deterministic:
• Capacitor µF is 22% below nameplate spec → replace capacitor → estimate generated. No vision AI needed.
• Suction pressure low + superheat high → XGBoost classifies as leak → estimate for recharge. No vision needed.
• Voltage drop 4.2V across terminal → loose terminal confirmed → estimate for retorque. No vision needed.
• Amp draw 18A vs nameplate RLA 12A → compressor over-amping → assessment estimate. No vision needed.

The reading tells you what happened. The nameplate tells you what should have happened. The gap is the diagnosis.
That gap drives the estimate. That is a rule-based system, not an AI system — and it works confidently for the majority of calls.

The AI vision is the evidence layer that makes the PDF compelling and catches the cases a reading can't reach (drain clog, dirty filter, corrosion). The reading is the product.
📊 Recommendation Models — Reading-Based, No Vision Needed
If a capacitor reads 18% below spec but hasn't failed yet → app recommends: "Schedule preventive replacement before peak summer — this capacitor will likely fail within one season."
That is a threshold rule, not a trained model. Powerful enough to generate upsell recommendations on every single call.

⚠️ CRITICAL GAP

Without Task #9, techs can skip the pressure step and get an estimate with no pressure data — XGBoost never fires.
Gemini — Complex Reasoning (Multi-Input Situations)
Not classification — reasoning through ambiguous situations where pressure + photo + context all need to be weighed. Cannot be replaced by rules.
5 SITUATIONS WHERE GEMINI IS THE ONLY TOOL
#17 vs #14 tiebreak — Overcharge and dirty coil both show high discharge. Gemini reasons through subcooling level, coil photo, airflow, ambient temp to separate them. XGBoost alone misclassifies a percentage of these.

#13 Ductwork Leak — No single reading or photo confirms this. Gemini weighs normal pressures, warm supply air, delta-T across zones, system history.

#18 Undersizing — Square footage, insulation, window count, duct condition, Houston climate. Multi-factor synthesis.

#16 Path B (no camera) — Visual photo + point thermometer readings + voltage drop. None individually confirms. Gemini connects them.

#7 Error Code (no brand DB) — When Task #12 isn't complete, Gemini reasons through model series + symptom history + LED pattern.
The Honest Count
Out of 19 cards, existing models give real value on roughly 8.
Cards #1, #2, #3, #4, #5, #6, #11 — the most common Phase 1 calls — get zero AI assistance from existing models.
Those cards run purely on the tech entering a reading and the app comparing it against a threshold rule.

That is not a failure. Threshold rules are fast, reliable, and zero latency. But it means the "AI" in those cards right now is basically an if-else statement, not a model. The product works. The AI layer will make it more compelling over time as training data accumulates from beta calls.
