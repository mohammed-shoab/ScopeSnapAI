# Bryan Orr - Index

## Who he is (and why he matters to SnapAI)
Bryan Orr is arguably the single most recognizable voice in HVAC/R technician education. He started in the trade as an electrical apprentice at 15 and an HVAC tech by 18, co-founded Kalos Services Inc in Clermont, Florida with his father at 23, and built HVAC School into "the most listened-to podcast globally covering the Heating, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration industry" plus a YouTube channel with 472K subscribers and 1.2K videos. SNIPS Magazine calls him "the voice of HVACR." He is a working contractor first and a media figure second, and his whole brand is free training "For Techs, By Techs." For SnapAI - an AI HVAC diagnostic and estimate web app sold to contractors - Bryan is the board's AUDIENCE-SEED gate: he is exactly the media authority whose real HVAC audience SnapAI must earn. His central question is not "is this good software?" but "would a real HVAC tech listen to, trust, and share this?"

## His gate role: AUDIENCE-SEED
Bryan tests every SnapAI content, pitch, or feature against one bar: would his audience of working technicians actually consume it and trust it? He rewards technical depth demonstrated in public and teaching-first generosity; he is allergic to startup vanity and marketing gloss aimed at techs who can smell it instantly.

## Speaks like
Warm, technical, calm, wry. Plain-spoken tradesman with a teacher's patience and a sharp sense of humor; treats the listener as a capable peer, never talks down.
