# Bryan Orr - Canonical Works

## HVAC School (podcast, YouTube, and free-training platform) - his flagship
HVAC School is Bryan's defining work and the reason he sits on the board. He founded it "to be free training HVAC/R across many mediums, For Techs, By Techs" (SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/bryan-orr/), and has "been involved in HVAC training for over 13 years" (SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/bryan-orr/). It spans a podcast described as "the most listened-to podcast globally covering the Heating, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration industry" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/hear) and a YouTube channel with "472K subscribers" and "1.2K videos" whose mission is "real, applicable training for techs and contractors as well as general education resource for the industry" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.youtube.com/@HVACS). Representative content spans pure craft to advanced theory: "Capillary Tube Repair and Brazing Class," "Why Compressors Fail: Diagnosis, Replacement and Prevention," "EEVMate Tool: Testing Electronic Expansion Valves in Mini Splits and VRF Systems," and "Why a TXV instead of a TEV?.. or a CSV?" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.youtube.com/@HVACS; SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/category/podcast-episodes/). The catalog reaches from beginner craft ("HVAC Troubleshooting 101") to instrumentation-heavy diagnostics ("Refrigeration Communication Troubleshooting Using Oscilloscopes") and data-driven method ("Symposium - What Good Techs Do Different W/ Data"). For SnapAI this is the single most important asset in Bryan's world: it IS the HVAC technician audience, concentrated - and a single genuine appearance reaches it at scale.

## Kalos Services Inc (his contracting business) - the credibility root
Bryan co-founded Kalos with his father when he was young - "At 23, I co-founded Kalos Services Inc" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/about); the SNIPS profile notes he started it with his dad "when he was 17" (SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/bryan-orr/). The business has served "Central Florida since 2005" and is "Family-owned - Licensed and insured" out of 236 Hatteras Ave, Clermont, FL, holding multiple state licenses (EC0001523, CAC1814620, CBC0557190, CFC1434632) (SOURCE_URL: https://www.kalosflorida.com/about/). It is a full-trade commercial contractor - "a one-stop-shop for commercial construction" with in-house HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing, electrical, and construction (SOURCE_URL: https://www.kalosflorida.com/about/). Its "Four Pillars" are Community Involvement and Social Good, Training/Education ("knowledge is power at Kalos"), Humanity (family-owned, founders with "over 100 years of combined experience"), and All the Trades in One Place; community work includes going to Haiti to teach young people the trades (SOURCE_URL: https://www.kalosflorida.com/about/). Kalos is why Bryan's media authority is real and not performative: he "regularly helps his team train and troubleshoot as a working contractor" and serves as vice-president of service (SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/bryan-orr/). His authority is earned on the truck, not just the mic.

## "Unconformed" (book) - his manifesto
Bryan's book "Unconformed" is "my manifesto against stagnation. It's for anyone feeling stuck, a guide to carving your own path and embracing 'unstuckable' growth" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/about; SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/read). It generalizes his personal theme - getting "unstuck" and helping others do the same - beyond HVAC into a broader message about growth and self-direction. HVAC School even carries a dedicated "Bryan Orr Books" content category, signaling that authorship is a standing part of his platform, not a one-off (SOURCE_URL: http://www.hvacrschool.com/category/podcast-episodes/).

## His other podcasts and audio work - the broader media builder
Bryan is a serious audio producer, not just an HVAC host. He runs "Dialed In" (with Matt Moreman, on "obsession, craft, and connection") and "Career Cafe" (career stories for Career Source Central Florida) (SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/hear). Earlier shows where he "cut his teeth" include "WOW Small Business," "Mantastic Voyage," "The Life in The Villages Podcast," "Podcast Movement Sessions," and a narrative series "Accelerator" tracking Orlando tech startups. He has also "produced and worked on many other podcasts and documentary projects, including NPR's 'Marketplace,' WNYC's 'More Perfect,' '99% Invisible,' 'Snap Judgment,' and 'The Startup Podcast' by Gimlet Media" (SOURCE_URL: https://www.bryanorr.com/hear). This depth of media craft - across some of the most respected names in audio - is why his content bar is high and why he can tell a polished pitch from a sloppy one instantly.
