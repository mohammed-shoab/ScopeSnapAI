Card #21 (Heat Exchanger) — PERMANENTLY EXCLUDED — never to be built in SnapAI (Shoab, 2026-07-06)
This is a permanent no-build, not a hold. SnapAI does not diagnose heat exchangers, combustion, or carbon monoxide; any such concern is referred to a separate licensed in-person safety inspection. Legal basis (Alfred, US/TX): a false-negative on a cracked heat exchanger carries carbon-monoxide personal-injury / wrongful-death exposure that is categorically beyond any mechanical fault card; back-side and hairline seam cracks are not reliably detectable from a photo, so a "clean" output would be an unsupportable safety representation; and the risk is uninsurable without a specific rider. Structural exclusion is the conservative, defensible posture and matches the PDF safety-inspection disclaimer in the legal-safe wordings.
