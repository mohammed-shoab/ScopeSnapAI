## Frameworks library

### Charlotte / $10M brand playbook (CharCharms, Wall Candy)
- White space / pattern disruption: 10 percent different is enough; mood-board competitors, find the unused color/format.
- Viral-worthy moment built INTO the product (whipped-cream sunscreen). SnapAI equivalent: the deterministic receipt.
- Content that works: reply-to-comment, us-vs-them comparison (mild controversy), content-in-public (film 100, post the funny). "Unexpected" stops the scroll.
- TikTok = discovery; Instagram = nurture the niche; LinkedIn = B2B/retail buyers (founder DMs everyone).
- Community IS the moat. Her verdict on Stanley: a trend without community fizzles. Founder-visible, parasocial, weekly calls with top customers.
- Founder is first creator/affiliate; 3 posts/day. Track view->sale (15M views, 0 sales = kill it).

### Kallaway V1 - three shifts
- AI-Enabled Creativity: use AI to kill boring tasks (research, topic-mining, hook drafts); KEEP the human reactive take as the sauce.
- Social Commerce (monetization 3.0): 4 tailwinds - Instagram Shops (30 links/video), agentic social commerce (auto-DM selling), collapsing product-build cost, AI click-to-buy. The skill of driving attention is about to 5-10x.
- The Storytelling Era: trend-chasing/overstim editing dying; premium storytelling wins. 5 pillars - characters, pacing, narrative arcs, world-building, tension.

### Kallaway V2 - six rules
- Flashbang Brain: viewers have ZERO medium-term memory - only 30-sec micro-memory + long-term facial recognition. Every video is first-and-last contact; drive each to its own CTA/lead-magnet. No serialized arcs. (Cats survive via the facial-recognition bucket + self-contained videos.)
- Become the Gold Standard: best-in-category (interesting, novel, crystal-clear, polished). Own the minimum-sized niche aligned to the offer. Filter: does this move the buyer one step toward the outcome they want?
- Explosivity Factor: engineered content can explode (Kumar 0->1M in 5 days), but outliers are not the plan; Lindy effect - consistency wins; only real loss is stopping.
- The Stealing Economy: your content will be ripped. Make it hard to steal - IRL/sets, longer-form, N-of-1 personal-experience ideas. Remix principles = good; copy word-for-word = bad.
- Personal Brand Differentiation: 3 levers - visual differentiation, originality, tactical implementability. Transpositioning = remix formats from OUTSIDE your niche (highest ROI).
- Formats and Surfaces: Kallaway ranks Instagram king (comment->click->lead), YouTube king for lead-gen/sales, and says "almost no value in TikTok unless you are a consumer commodity product or a yapping/personal-brand play." AI-dilution wave moves left->right (short text most at risk -> live streams safest).

### Kallaway V3 - The Illusion of Novelty (master script skeleton)
Make ANY boring topic (his example: root canals) addictive. Addictive = relevant + novel + interesting. 5 steps:
1. New Reveal + Outcome Mapping - reveal a new angle on an old thing, tie it to an outcome the viewer wants.
2. Contrast Framing - position against the old belief ("everyone thinks X, but actually Y"); the belief-gap freezes the scroll.
3. Urgency (Turbo Button) - optional; recency bias ("this just dropped"); never fake it.
4. Bullseye Proof - proof closest to the viewer's life wins. Trust ladder: one-to-one mimic (best) -> warm crowd -> third-party study (weakest).
5. Protect the Illusion - commit to the storyline; deliver as a whispered secret (gossip whisperer), never announce like an ad.
Use this as the spine of every SnapAI short.
